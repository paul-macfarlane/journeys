# AC-5 — crossing arrow pairs on the seeded case-3 map, measured from the rendered paths

Measurement (identical in both runs): every arrow's `path.react-flow__edge-path`
inside a `[data-choice-id]` wrapper is sampled with `getPointAtLength` every
4 path units (at least 16 samples) in flow coordinates, and the polylines are
counted with `countCrossingPairs` (`src/lib/graph/crossings.ts`): two arrows
cross when their paths properly intersect farther than 6 units from either
arrow's ends, and a pair counts once.

| Build | Boxes | Arrows | Sampled paths | Crossing pairs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ticket 09's canvas at `f24a106` (baseline) | 36 | 50 | 50 | 15 (before fit view) / 15 (after fit view) |
| this work package at the captured commit | 36 | 50 | 50 | 5 |

Baseline run: a detached worktree of `f24a106` with the branch's
`src/lib/graph/crossings.ts` copied in and a throwaway spec
`e2e/crossings-baseline.spec.ts` (same setup as `canvas-case-3-map`: sign in,
create a Project and a Journey, write the case-3 document into the Draft,
reload, wait until every box is inside the canvas and none overlap), run with
`pnpm exec playwright test e2e/crossings-baseline.spec.ts` against the
`journeys_e2e` database on 2026-09-21. Printed lines:

    baseline f24a106: nodes=36 edges=50 sampledPaths=50 crossingPairsBeforeFit=15 crossingPairsAfterFit=15
    baseline f24a106: nodes=36 edges=50 sampledPaths=50 crossingPairs=15

Nothing from that worktree was committed; it was removed afterwards.

Current run: `canvas-case-3-map` in `e2e/canvas.spec.ts` (part of `pnpm test:e2e`,
see `dod-1-e2e.txt`), which asserts the count is below 15 and prints:

    case-3 crossing pairs: f24a106 baseline=15 now=5

Dimming (the second half of AC-5) is proven by `canvas-selection-dims-arrows`
in the same run: with a box selected, every arrow attached to it carries
`data-emphasis="attached"` and every other arrow `data-emphasis="dimmed"`.
